About the Summit

From Discovery to Global Impact

The GeneX Commercialization Summit 2027 addresses the critical friction points preventing human-led academic genetic research from becoming scalable commercial enterprises.

While scientific discovery remains exceptionally strong, translating benchtop genetic platforms into commercial therapies often fails due to complex IP landscapes, biomanufacturing scale-up bottlenecks, intricate regulatory frameworks, and unsustainable pricing models.

Over three structured days in Melbourne’s world-class Parkville Biomedical Precinct, GeneX 2027 brings together leaders across genome engineering, technology transfer, regulatory affairs, bioprocessing, and biopharma venture capital to bridge the gap between academic research and commercial application.
