Biography & Focus

Professor John Rasko AO is an internationally recognised physician–scientist and leader in gene and cell therapy, regenerative medicine, and molecular biotechnology. A clinical haematologist, pathologist, and scientist, he has pioneered the global introduction of gene and cell therapies and regenerative medicine. From 1999 to 2024, he directed Cell & Molecular Therapies at Royal Prince Alfred Hospital and the Gene & Stem Cell Therapy Program at The University of Sydney, establishing acclaimed programs to integrate research, manufacturing, and clinical translation.

Professor Rasko chaired Australia’s Gene Technology Technical Advisory Committee (GTTAC) from 2008 to 2026, overseeing national scientific advice relating to genetically modified organisms and emerging biotechnologies, and serves on the World Health Organization International Nomenclature Committee. He co-founded the Australasian Gene and Cell Therapy Society and served as President of the International Society for Cell and Gene Therapy (2018–2020), contributing to global policy and standards for safe and effective gene-based medicines.

At GeneX 2027, Prof. Rasko chairs discussions bringing together academic researchers, clinical leaders, regulatory experts, and industry partners to explore global pathways for translating genetic discoveries into safe, scalable clinical and commercial applications."
