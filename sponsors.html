Why Partner with GeneX 2027?

Connect your organization with the people, technologies, and partnerships shaping the future of biopharma commercialization.

The GeneX Commercialization Summit 2027 brings together top academic researchers, biotech founders, venture capitalists, CDMO executives, and biopharma leaders across the Asia-Pacific region and beyond.

By sponsoring or exhibiting at GeneX 2027, your organization directly connects with key decision-makers who are solving critical bottlenecks in IP protection, preclinical-to-clinical regulatory approval, biomanufacturing scale-up (CMC), and health economics.

Targeted Audience

Place your technology, services, or platforms in front of academic spin-offs, life-science VCs, biomanufacturing leaders, and regulatory experts.

Thought Leadership

Position your executive team as category experts through keynote presentations, panel discussions, and workshop hosting.

Qualified B2B Matchmaking

Engage in dedicated 1-on-1 networking sessions, exhibition hall showcases, and social receptions.
