Melbourne Weather in September (Early Spring)

September marks the arrival of spring in Melbourne. The city begins to warm up after winter, bringing blooming gardens, longer daylight hours, and pleasant conditions for outdoor networking.

Average Temperature

Daily highs typically range around 16°C to 18°C (60°F to 64°F), while overnight lows dip to around 8°C to 10°C (46°F to 50°F).

What to Expect

Melbourne is famous for its variable weather ("four seasons in one day"). While you can expect crisp, sunny spring days, occasional brisk winds or passing showers can occur.

What to Pack

We recommend bringing layers, smart-casual blazers, cardigans, or a stylish overcoat for cooler mornings and evenings, alongside a light travel umbrella just in case!
